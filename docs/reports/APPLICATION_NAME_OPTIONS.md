# EduSynk — Creative Application Naming & Rebranding Options

**Date**: September 10, 2026  
**Target Application**: Peer-to-Peer Student Skill Exchange & Collaborative Learning Platform  
**Purpose**: Evaluate original, memorable, and student-focused brand names to replace or evolve the current name **SkillNexus**.

---

## 1. Naming Evaluation Criteria

To ensure the new name resonates with university students while projecting academic credibility and modern product design, candidate names are evaluated against 6 dimensions:

1. **Relevance**: Direct association with two-way skill sharing (teaching & learning).
2. **Brevity & Cadence**: Short (1–3 syllables), easy to spell, and easy to pronounce.
3. **Student Identity**: Approachable, peer-focused, energetic, and non-intimidating.
4. **Distinctiveness**: Stands apart from generic corporate LMS platforms or job portals.
5. **International Adaptability**: Renders cleanly across English and global UIs.
6. **Domain & Brand Feel**: Suits a modern web application (.app, .edu, .io, .com).
